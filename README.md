## Concept Overview

This system is designed to address key challenges faces by the public in the transport sector. Many commuters experience uncertainty, delays, and inconvenience due to limited access to reliable transport information and services

# Problem Statement
The current public transport system experiences several challenges:
  * Limited or no access to accurate fare information
  * Inconsistent and unreliable transport services
  * Passengers waiting long for departure and people waiting long hours for transport arrivals
  * Conflicts among passengers over seat availability
# Proposed solution
To address these challenges , the system introduces the following features:
  1. Fare notification system
    users receive accurate fare information immediately after selecting their destination based on their location.
  2. Seat booking system
    passengers can reserve seats in advance by booking, reducing disputes and ensuring convenience.
  3. Bus schedule tracking
    users can view real-time or schedule arrival and departure times, allowing for better planning and reduced waiting times.

# Conclusion
This system aims to improve the efficiency, reliability, and overall user experience of public transport by providing  accessibility and timely information, as well as convenient booking featuress.
